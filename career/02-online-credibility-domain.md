## Key concepts

1. **Domain knowledge is the moat** — anyone can run XGBoost; not everyone knows what RFM means in retail or what churn looks like in telecom.
2. **Pick one or two domains and read deeply** — finance, healthcare, retail/CPG, marketing, supply chain, telecom, energy.
3. **Read the trade press, not just papers** — The Economist's industry reports, McKinsey/BCG insights, industry blogs.
4. **Vocabulary matters** — say "AOV," "LTV," "RFM," "ARPU," "DAU/MAU" correctly in interviews.
5. **Public datasets in your domain** — proves you can apply skills there.

## Domain starter packs

### Retail / CPG
- KPIs: revenue, units sold, AOV, basket size, gross margin, conversion, return rate
- Concepts: assortment, planogram, demand forecasting, inventory turns, shrinkage
- Datasets: AtliQ Hardware (Codebasics!), Walmart Kaggle, Online Retail UCI

### Finance
- KPIs: NPL, default rate, RAROC, ROE, NIM
- Concepts: credit scoring, fraud detection, AML, time-series forecasting
- Datasets: Lending Club, Credit Card Fraud (Kaggle)

### Marketing / Growth
- KPIs: CAC, LTV, ROAS, CTR, conversion rate, churn
- Concepts: attribution, A/B testing, RFM segmentation, funnel analysis
- Datasets: Google Analytics sample, Olist e-commerce

### Healthcare
- KPIs: readmission rate, length of stay, cost per case
- Concepts: ICD codes, EHR, HIPAA, patient cohorts
- Datasets: MIMIC-III/IV (requires registration), CDC public data

### Supply chain / logistics
- KPIs: OTIF, fill rate, days of inventory, lead time, forecast accuracy
- Concepts: demand planning, S&OP, route optimization
- Datasets: DataCo, Brazilian E-commerce (delivery data)

## External resources

- McKinsey/BCG/Bain insights pages — free articles by industry
- Trade journals (Adweek, Modern Healthcare, Supply Chain Dive)
- Industry-specific subreddits + LinkedIn groups
- Codebasics' AtliQ business case studies (do all of them)

## Practice projects

1. Pick a domain → pick one public dataset → write 10 business questions a stakeholder might ask → answer all 10 in a notebook.
2. Replicate a published industry case study with public data.
3. Interview someone in that domain (LinkedIn cold outreach) — write up what surprised you.
