## Key concepts

1. **Working in public > working in private** for learners — feedback, accountability, network compound over time.
2. **Cadence beats perfection** — 1 short post a week beats 1 polished post a quarter.
3. **Post the work, not the wrapper** — show the chart, the bug, the fix. Don't wait until everything is "ready."
4. **Document, don't market** — the audience is your future self + people doing the same thing.
5. **Two-way street** — comment on others, don't just broadcast.

## Cadence

- **LinkedIn**: 2 posts/week minimum
- **X / Twitter**: optional, daily fragments
- **GitHub**: at least 3 commits/week
- **Blog (Medium / Hashnode / personal site)**: 1 deep post / 2 weeks
